A monostable has one stable state (Q = 0). A rising edge on its Trigger input kicks Q HIGH for a set time, the pulse width tw, and then Q drops back on its own. The timing resistor Rx and capacitor Cx set tw. Press and hold TRIGGER (or tap the push button in the circuit) and watch the Q LED: it stays lit for tw no matter how long you hold the button. Change Rx or Cx and try again.

Why R and C set the pulse width
When the IC triggers, Cx charges through Rx. Q stays HIGH until the capacitor voltage reaches the IC's switching level — then the pulse ends. Drag the sliders: a bigger R or C charges more slowly, reaches the switching level later, and makes a longer pulse. The faint trace is the setting before your last change.
