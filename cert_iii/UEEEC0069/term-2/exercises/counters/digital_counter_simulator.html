Mod-8 Up-Counter · 74193 · Simulator

A 74193 is a 4-bit up/down counter. Here it counts up on the clock input CU, and the QD output (the most significant bit) is wired back to the C (clear) input. Click CLK to send a clock pulse (or AUTO CLK to keep sending them) and watch the count climb. When it reaches 8 (1000), QD goes HIGH — see where that 1 travels, and what it does when it gets there.
