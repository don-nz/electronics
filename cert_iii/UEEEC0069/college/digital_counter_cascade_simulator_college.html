Two 74193 Counters Chained · 8-Bit · Simulator

A single 74193 counts 0 – 15 (4 bits, 24 = 16 states). To count higher, chain two: IC1 counts the clock (the low 4 bits) and its CO (carry out) is wired to IC2's CU clock input, so IC2 counts once for every 16 pulses. Together they make an 8-bit counter: 4 + 4 = 8 bits, so 28 = 256 states (0 – 255). Click CLK to send pulses, or use Go to 14 to jump to just before the first carry and watch what happens at 15 → 0.
