A three-input logic circuit, Z = A·B·C + B·C, built on a breadboard the way you would build it in the lab. Click the A, B and C switches and watch the levels on every wire and pin; the logic indicator shows Z (red for 1, green for 0, like the college trainer). Tap any hole to see which holes it is connected to, and tap any IC pin to see what it is.
