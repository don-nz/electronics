An unclocked SR latch follows one rule at every instant:
S = 1, R = 0Set — Q goes (or stays) HIGH
S = 0, R = 1Reset — Q goes (or stays) LOW
S = 0, R = 0Hold — Q stays exactly where it was
S = 1, R = 1Invalid — never shown here
