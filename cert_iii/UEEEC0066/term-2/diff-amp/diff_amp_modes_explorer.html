A differential amplifier has two modes of operation. In differential mode the two inputs carry equal but opposite signals and the amplifier gives a big output. In common mode they carry the identical signal and it hardly responds at all. Any real pair of inputs is a mix of the two. Use the sliders (or the presets) to drive In 1 and In 2 and watch the tail node and the outputs. A negative amplitude is the same wave turned upside down.
