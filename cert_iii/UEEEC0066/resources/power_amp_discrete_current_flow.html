Working it out, step by step

    The numbers come from a model with typical part values (small transistors VBE ≈ 0.62 V at 1 mA, β = 200; power transistors VBE ≈ 0.55 V at 10 mA, β = 50). Each transistor's card gives its Av: the output terminal ÷ the input, collector for Q4 (vc ÷ vb) and Q1 (vc ÷ the difference between the two bases), emitter for the emitter followers. A real board will read a little differently, but every reading should move the same way when a switch changes.
